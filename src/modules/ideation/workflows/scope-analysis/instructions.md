# 📏 Scope Analysis - Workflow Instructions

## Overview

This workflow helps you honestly assess your project scope and match your ambition to your resources.

**Time:** 20-30 minutes  
**Output:** `scope-assessment.md`

---

## Why Scope Matters

80% of indie games never finish. The #1 reason? Scope too big.

Better to ship a small, polished game than abandon a big one.

---

## Step 1: Resource Assessment

### Time
- Hours per week you can dedicate?
- Target completion date?
- Total available hours?

### Team
- Solo or team?
- Team members' skills?
- Part-time or full-time?

### Budget
- Development budget?
- Asset budget?
- Marketing budget?

---

## Step 2: Scope Tiers

Define three versions:

### MVP (Minimum Viable Product)
The smallest game that proves the concept works.

### Target Scope
What you realistically aim to ship.

### Dream Scope
What you'd make with unlimited resources.

---

## Step 3: Scope Recommendation

Based on your resources, I'll recommend:
- Which scope tier is realistic
- What features to prioritize
- What can be cut or saved for updates
