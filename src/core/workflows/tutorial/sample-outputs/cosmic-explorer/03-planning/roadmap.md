# Cosmic Explorer — Roadmap

*Sample. Sprint 5 tutorial.*

## Timeline

- **Month 1:** Drift mechanic prototype (Epic 1)
- **Month 2:** Pulse mechanic + first level grey-box (Epics 2, 3)
- **Month 3:** Level flow, save, core music (Epic 4)
- **Month 4-5:** Remaining 12 rooms, visual polish
- **Month 6:** Audio polish, bug fixes, release

## Milestones

- **M1:** Drift prototype playable (end of month 1)
- **M2:** Airlock level complete, vertical slice (end of month 2)
- **M3:** All mechanics feature-complete (end of month 3)
- **M4 (beta):** All rooms playable, placeholder art (end of month 4)
- **M5 (alpha):** Art complete (end of month 5)
- **M6 (ship):** Release candidate (end of month 6)

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Drift feel takes too long to tune | High | Prototype by week 2 of month 1 |
| Scope creep in room count | Medium | Hard-cap at 15 rooms |
| Audio polish is last-minute | Low | Start ambient score in M3 |
