# Cosmic Explorer — Concept Brief

*Sample document produced by the Ideation team (Sparky + Ivy).
Part of the GD-KS tutorial. Not a real project.*

## Core Concept

A lost astronaut navigates a derelict space station using
low-gravity mobility and light puzzle mechanics. Quiet, atmospheric,
introspective — not a shooter.

## Target Audience

Players 18-35 who enjoy introspective puzzle games in the spirit of
Gris, Journey, or Firewatch. Tolerant of slow pacing, drawn to
aesthetic immersion. Plays in 30-minute sessions.

## Hook

"What if exploring a dead space station felt like solving a quiet
poem?" — every room tells a story through environment, not dialogue.

## Core Verb

**Drift** — players move in low-gravity with momentum-based control.
Not running, not walking. Intentionally unfamiliar, rewards
patience, creates vulnerability.

## Supporting Mechanics

- **Anchor** — latch onto a surface to arrest drift and regain
  control. Physical intention matters; you can't just freeze in space.
- **Resonance Pulse** — brief sound emission once per room,
  revealing hidden paths visually. Limited to prevent trivial puzzle
  solving.

## Reference Games

- **Gris** — art direction, silent narrative
- **Journey** — pacing, emotional arc, no dialogue
- **The Witness** — environmental storytelling density
- **Tacoma** — space-station atmosphere
- **Inside** — minimal UI, trust the player

## Rough Scope

- Solo dev, 6 months
- ~2 hours of critical-path gameplay
- 12-15 hand-crafted rooms
- 2D pixel art, atmospheric
- No voice acting, ambient score + reactive SFX only
