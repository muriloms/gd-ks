# Cosmic Explorer — Design Pillars

*Sample. Sprint 5 tutorial.*

## Pillar 1: Weightlessness as Mechanic

**Definition:** Low gravity isn't a constraint we apologize for — it
IS the game. Every system (movement, camera, audio, UI) leans into
the sensation of floating.

**Implications:**
- No double jumps, no wall runs — drift only.
- Camera slightly lags player to emphasize momentum.
- UI elements drift subtly to reinforce the world's physics.

**If cut:** lose game identity.

---

## Pillar 2: Environmental Storytelling

**Definition:** The story is told through rooms, not words. Object
placement, lighting, broken tech, scribbled notes — never dialogue.

**Implications:**
- Each room has a "narrative prop" (e.g. abandoned meal, open
  diary).
- Lighting conveys mood (cool blue = isolation, warm amber = past).
- Audio replaces dialogue: hums, creaks, wind through broken seals.

**If cut:** becomes a generic puzzle platformer.

---

## Pillar 3: Quiet Moments Matter

**Definition:** Pacing rewards stillness. Not every room has a
puzzle. Some rooms exist just to breathe.

**Implications:**
- At least 3 of 15 rooms have no interactive challenge.
- Ambient score must be layered so silence feels intentional.
- No timer, no urgency UI.

**If cut:** pacing collapses into constant problem-solving, loses
poetic weight.
